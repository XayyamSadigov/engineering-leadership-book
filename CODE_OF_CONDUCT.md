# Code of conduct

This project is a book about leading people well. Its contributors are expected to behave accordingly.

## Expected

- Disagree about ideas with evidence and reasons. Disagreement is welcome; it is how the book improves.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect that contributors bring different cultures, languages, and levels of experience.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any project space or directed at contributors elsewhere.
- Discriminatory language or conduct of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the repository owner's
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, and may warn,
suspend, or ban contributors whose conduct breaches this code.

This code is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which
projects needing a fuller policy may adopt directly.
