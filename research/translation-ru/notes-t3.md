## Conventions applied

- City names in Russian (Миннеаполис, Богота), following `cast.ru.md`; `year-in-brief.ru.md` once writes
  «из Bogotá» in Latin. The maintainer may want one form everywhere.
- Team names used as subjects take «команда» where Russian needs a gender («команда Orders внесла»).
- Table cells: all numbers copied verbatim; English words inside number cells ("about $53,000") were
  translated («около $53,000»), numbers untouched.
- No-break spaces inserted after numbers before Russian words in prose (not in tables or headings).

- Code blocks: ch17's ```text throughput history had its labels translated (numbers and decimal points
  kept as in English, like a table); spreadsheet formulas in the ch17 tip stay verbatim.
- The structural check (headings and anchors, table numbers, links, exercise labels, admonitions,
  Mermaid count) matches the English for all six chapters; `tools/validate_book.py` passes.
