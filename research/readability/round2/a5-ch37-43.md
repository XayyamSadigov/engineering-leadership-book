## Patterns across your scope
- **Shorthand for events from earlier chapters in tables**: Ch 38 example, Ch 39 decision log, Ch 40 period table, Ch 41 evidence tables, Ch 43 self-assessment. Book-wide rule: a table cell may reference an earlier Northstar event only with a plain-words clause saying what happened. Any case table whose rows need more than one other chapter to read should be cut to the rows that teach the chapter's method.
- **Names packed into table cells**: Ch 37 calendars, Ch 38 scorecard, Ch 41 plans. Rule: tables carry roles or counts; names go in the prose, and `cast.md` is linked once per chapter. No chapter in scope links `cast.md` today.
- **Glossary entries that exist but go unlinked**: severity, error budget, renewal commitments, Core, release train, percentile forecast, ADR-0001, INC numbers. Rule: link each glossary term at its first use in every chapter, not only in the chapter that owns it, and especially in the first chapter of a part.
- **Cross-references that stand in for content**: "Chapter 2's five levels" (Ch 41), "four areas (Chapter 4)" (Ch 40 line 244), "the cadence Chapter 5 recommends" (Ch 37 line 354), "value over duration (Chapter 15)" (Ch 38 line 429). Rule: when a chapter applies a tool from another chapter, restate it in one line or one small table, then link.
- **Repetition, mostly handled well**: the skip-level rule (Ch 40/41), the mum effect (Ch 39/40), and corridor decisions (Ch 37/39) are each stated once and cross-referenced. Two real overlaps remain. The director's calendar is reconstructed in both Ch 37 (16.3 recurring hours) and Ch 40 (14 hours excluding skip-levels), which a reader must reconcile alone. And the same one-sentence Northstar recap ("30 to 21") opens all seven chapters. Rule: Ch 40 should say explicitly that its forum figure is Ch 37's minus skip-levels.
- **Small numeric inconsistencies that cause doubt**: 47-hour week (Ch 37, Ch 41) against 48-hour week (Ch 40); 77 against 80 engineers. Rule: state the basis once per chapter.
- **Part ordering**: Part VIII (Ch 37–39) is written entirely from the director's seat before Ch 40 defines the role. Either add a two-line "this part assumes the director role described in Chapter 40" note to the Part VIII openings, or move Part IX's Ch 40 ahead of Part VIII in the reading roadmap.
- **Several kinds of register**: decision log, open-decisions list, change register, commitments list, technology risk register, priced debt register, interest map, escalation map. Each is reasonable alone; together they blur. Rule: one table in Ch 37 or Ch 39 listing the director's standing artifacts, what each holds, and which forum reads it.
