# Download the book

The whole book is available as an e-book and as a print-ready PDF, built from the same sources as this
website:

- [EPUB](https://xayyamsadigov.github.io/engineering-leadership-book/downloads/engineering-leadership-handbook.epub) for e-readers and reading apps (Apple Books,
  Kobo, Google Play Books, Calibre). Tables and diagrams reflow to the screen.
- [PDF](https://xayyamsadigov.github.io/engineering-leadership-book/downloads/engineering-leadership-handbook.pdf) at 6 × 9 inches, with page numbers and a
  clickable table of contents, for reading on a tablet or printing.

Both files are rebuilt automatically every time the book changes, so they always match the website.
Each one says on its first page which commit it was built from.

The downloads contain every chapter, template, project, the Northstar case pages, and the
bibliography, with all worked solutions gathered in a final "Solutions" part. Pages that exist only on
the website, such as the Northstar data files, appear in the downloads as plain text rather than links.

Like the website, every chapter is awaiting human review: the downloads say so once, at the front,
rather than on every chapter. The text is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
