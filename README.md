# The Engineering Leadership Handbook

**From Senior Engineer to Engineering Manager, Director, VP Engineering, and CTO**

An open, evidence-informed textbook on building, leading, scaling, and transforming software
engineering organizations. Sixty chapters take you from your first team to the CTO's seat, with
one fictional company, Northstar Technologies, followed through a year of recovery as the running
case.

## How this book is made

The book was written with AI agents (Claude) working under binding authoring rules
([`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)): every source verified on a fetched page and recorded in
[`research/bibliography.yaml`](research/bibliography.yaml), no invented quotations or statistics,
evidence labels on every claim, and automated checks in CI. It has not yet had a full human review
of its citations; corrections are the most valuable contribution. See
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) to report an error or to build the book locally.
